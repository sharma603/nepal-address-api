Third-party address data attribution

Names, local-level types and ward counts: Local States Nepal
https://github.com/sagautam5/local-states-nepal

Postal directory transcription: Nepal Reference Data
https://github.com/Forgesaroj/nepal-reference-data
Primary source: Government of Nepal, Department of Postal Services
https://gpo.gov.np/pages/postal-code-1259614658/
Upstream retrieval date: 2026-09-27.
The government-derived data has no separately asserted data license in the
transcription repository. Postal codes are not census/MoFAGA administrative codes.
Village/tole coverage is incomplete.
See api/v1/coverage.json and docs/DATA_SOURCES.md for revisions and limitations.
